    Why a search gets its own exact campaign

    It has to pass all three: it is relevant to the product, it converts better than the market on the same search (index 1.1 or more, from SQP first, the search-term report second), and it has real volume (150+ searches in 12 weeks).

    Invest means the signal rests on 3 or more purchases: full budget, top of search +50%. Prove first means the search beats the market but on fewer than 3 purchases: $5 a day and +30% until it reaches 3 orders (then it moves up to Invest) or spends about 30 clicks with no order (then it drops to Trim). Prove first is not the same as the Test role, which is for searches with too few clicks to judge at all and bids Amazon's low.
